Compatibility

What survives a round trip between Kertas and Microsoft Office, what is partial, and what is not built yet. Saved files are checked by reopening them in independent tools before each release.

Works wellOpens, edits and saves without loss PartialKept, with a stated limit Not yetOn the roadmap or out of scope
W

Writer and .docx

Text, styles, lists, tables, imagesWorks well
Headers, footers, sections, page numbersWorks well
Track changes and commentsWorks wellIncluding replies and resolved comments.
EquationsWorks wellSaved as native Word equations, editable in Word.
Citations, bibliography, table of contentsWorks wellAPA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard and IEEE.
Form fields: text, tick box, dropdown, dateWorks wellSaved as Word content controls.
Shapes with text wrapWorks well
Floating text boxesPartialKept when saved; their position inside Kertas can differ slightly from Word.
Older formats (.doc, .rtf, .odt)PartialOpen for editing and save as .docx. Full .doc formatting needs LibreOffice installed.
Presentations (.pptx)Not yetOut of scope. Kertas focuses on documents, spreadsheets and PDFs.
S

Sheets and .xlsx

Formulas, number formats, multiple sheetsWorks well
Fonts, fills, borders, merges, frozen panesWorks well
ChartsWorks wellColumn, bar, line, area, pie, doughnut and scatter, saved as real Excel charts.
Conditional formatting and sparklinesWorks wellAlso kept when exporting to PDF.
Threaded commentsWorks wellSaved as Excel threaded comments with authors, plus a note copy that LibreOffice, WPS and older Excel can read.
Named rangesWorks wellSaved as Excel defined names.
Data validation, filters, slicersWorks well
PivotTables made in KertasWorks wellSaved as genuine Excel pivots that refresh.
PivotTables from ExcelPartialKept with their values. Kertas can edit pivots with up to two row fields and two values.
CSVWorks wellKeeps leading zeros, long numbers, the original encoding and separator.
Older formats (.xls, .ods)PartialOpen for editing and save as .xlsx.
Macros (VBA)Not yetMacros do not run in Kertas.
P

PDF

Viewing, search, annotationsWorks well
Merge, split, reorder, rotate, delete pagesWorks wellBookmarks are kept.
Filling and creating formsWorks well
Attached filesWorks wellFiles carried inside a PDF can be opened, saved, added and removed.
Certificate signaturesWorks wellReported valid by independent checkers; any later change breaks the signature.
RedactionWorks wellRemoves the content underneath, not just covers it.
Editing existing textPartialUses the original font where it is available; otherwise a close substitute.
OCR and conversion to Word and ExcelWorks wellAccuracy depends on scan quality.
Password-protected PDFsPartialOpen, protect and save with encryption. Permission limits set by the author (such as no printing) are removed when saved.
